Privacy Policy

What QiaoDesktop and this website do with your data — stated as plainly as we can manage.

Last updated 22 October 2026  ·  Covers the QiaoDesktop desktop app and qiaodesktop.com

The short version

The desktop app asks our site once a day whether a newer version exists. That request carries the version number and nothing else — nothing in it can tell two computers apart. No telemetry, no analytics, no crash reports. Everything else it knows stays in one folder on your own PC.

This site asks for no account and sets no cookies of its own. Our hosting provider keeps aggregate, cookieless visit counts.

Our two contact pages load a Tencent online-consultation widget, and that widget sets a few cookies. If you would rather avoid them, email us instead — the address is on that page.

1. What this policy covers

Two things: the QiaoDesktop desktop application for Windows (“the app”), and this website at qiaodesktop.com (“the site”). It does not cover any third-party site we link to.

2. The desktop application

2.1 It goes online for exactly one purpose: updates

At most once a day the app sends a version query to qiaodesktop.com to find out whether a newer release exists. The request carries two things only: the app’s current version number and the distribution channel (website or store). No device ID, no hardware fingerprint, no installation ID, no file paths or filenames, no record of what you did — nothing in that request can tell two computers apart. All we can tell from it is how many times a given version was queried on a given day, never who queried it.

That same request is the update check itself, not an analytics channel: we run no usage analytics, collect no crash reports and use no advertising identifiers. With the network disconnected the app works exactly the same, it just cannot tell you about a new version.

To turn it off: open Settings → General and clear “Check for updates daily”. Once it is off, the app sends no network requests at all — not a single byte.

2.2 What it stores, and where

All app data lives in %APPDATA%\QiaoDesktop\ on your own computer. Nothing in that folder is uploaded anywhere.

File or folderWhat it holds
qd_config.jsonYour boxes, their positions and sizes, panel and wallpaper settings, interface language, and the paths of folders you have mapped.
qd_notes.jsonText you type into sticky notes, stored per note in plain text.
shortcuts\Shortcut files you have dragged into a box.
debug\Error logs, written only when something fails — never during a normal start.

2.3 Why it reads your files

To draw icons, names and thumbnails inside a box, the app reads file and folder listings from your disk and extracts icons from the files themselves. A mapping box shows whatever sits in the folder you pointed it at. All of this happens locally and in memory, and produces nothing but pixels on your screen — file contents are never transmitted, copied elsewhere, or collected into any database.

2.4 Trial period and activation

The 15-day full-feature trial starts on first launch; only the start timestamp is written, into the config folder above. An activation code is verified entirely on your machine: the app checks its signature against a public key built into the program, then compares a hardware fingerprint so one code cannot be reused on a different computer. No activation request is sent to us, and we are not told when you activate.

2.5 Error logs

When something fails, the app may write a short plain-text log under debug\, which can include the file paths involved. Logs stay on your disk, are never uploaded, and you may delete them at any time.

2.6 Removing your data

Delete %APPDATA%\QiaoDesktop\ and every trace of app data is gone. Removing the app does not delete that folder for you, because it also holds your layout — delete it yourself when you want a clean slate.

3. This website

3.1 No account, no forms

There is no login, no sign-up, no comment box and no contact form. The site never asks for your name, address or phone number.

3.2 Cookies set by the site itself

None. Our pages store nothing in cookies, localStorage or sessionStorage. The live demo is a self-contained animation that records no events.

3.3 Cloudflare Web Analytics

The site is served by Cloudflare Pages, which adds a cookieless analytics beacon to every page. It measures aggregate page views, referrers, country and device type. Cloudflare states that Web Analytics uses no cookies, does not fingerprint visitors and does not track you across other sites, so it cannot single you out.

3.4 Contact pages: the Tencent chat widget

Our two contact pages load an online-consultation widget from Tencent Cloud (trtc.io). This is the only part of the site that does more than count page views. While loaded, it may:

  • set cookies whose names begin with sa_jssdk_ and sajssdk_, holding an anonymous visitor ID, a first-visit flag, and the traffic source and referrer you arrived with;
  • store a value named visitorId-… in localStorage so the widget recognises you on a later visit;
  • send requests to api.trtc.io and sc-rp.tencentcloud.com.

The widget is supplied and operated by Tencent, and what it does with data is governed by Tencent’s own privacy policy (linked below). We do not receive your IP address or browsing history from it: we see the chat messages you choose to send, and nothing more.

3.5 How to avoid the widget

Keep off the contact pages and none of the above happens — the widget is not loaded anywhere else. If you want to reach us without it, use the email address at the end of this page. You can also block third-party cookies or cross-site scripts in your browser; the rest of the site keeps working normally.

5. Email

If you email us we receive your address, your name if you sign with one, and whatever you write. We use it to answer you and to keep track of the issue. We do not add you to a mailing list, do not use it for marketing, and do not sell or share it. Ask us to delete the thread and we will.

6. Children

The app and the site are general-purpose desktop-organisation tools, not aimed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children — and since the app collects nothing at all, there is nothing for a child using it to hand over.

7. Your rights

Because we hold no data about you from the app, most requests can be satisfied by your own file manager. For anything that does reach us — an email you sent, a message you typed into the chat — you may ask us to show it, correct it or delete it, and we will. Write to the address below.

8. Changes to this policy

If the app or the site starts doing something new that affects privacy, this page is updated first and the date at the top changes. Significant changes are also noted in the app’s release notes.

9. Contact

Questions about this policy, or about data in general: contact@qiaodesktop.com. Our contact page lists what to include so we can answer quickly.
