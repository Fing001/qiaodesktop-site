Everything else, at a glance

The three above are the core operations; the rest you don’t need to study — just know where they are.

CapabilityNotes & where
Box shapesNormal, collapsed, iconized, docked, expanded. Collapsed leaves only a title bar; double-click empty space on the title bar to restore.
Dual file-panel mode“Explorer” (default) and “Mapping box” self-drawn view, switched in the mapping bar’s right-click “Switch file display panel”, not in Settings.
WallpaperImage and video wallpapers, manual switch anytime.
Note boxJot things right inside a desktop box; paste from clipboard supported.
Game modeTray menu “Game mode” or Ctrl+Alt+G to turn on manually, hides all boxes and edge hot-zones; doesn’t auto-detect game processes.
Backup & restoreSettings Center page 3, keeps the last 20 backups automatically, migrate your whole layout to a new PC.

About the trial: organizing, boxes, the mapping bar, the dock, the float hotkey, tray summon, game mode and backup/restore are all unrestricted by the trial and keep working after it expires. PRO unlocks the super edge, auto-sink as low as 0.3 s, holding Ctrl to temporarily unlock a locked box, dynamic/video wallpaper and scheduled wallpaper switching.
